1. In the matrix company, create a purchase order to the partner of the
   maquiladora, in the currency of the program, with the Incoterm, and
   confirm it.
2. In the maquiladora, the sale order is already there, with the maquila
   program, the CNIME contract (tab Maquila) and the export fiscal
   position. Produce, deliver and invoice it as usual.
3. Post the export invoice. The matrix receives the vendor bill, linked to
   the purchase order lines, with the Paraguayan document number and the
   NCM of each line.
